1. Primary Rules

The 10 primary rules approved by the school authorities.

01

Any student who physically attacks or fights with a teacher shall face a two-week suspension. Upon returning to school after the suspension, the student must be accompanied by his or her parent or guardian for a meeting with the school authorities.

02

Every student must belong to at least one school club or extracurricular group, such as Debating, Comedy, Drama, Press Club, Recitation, or Quiz.

03

Every student must keep their school uniform clean, neat, and properly ironed at all times. Students must maintain a decent and presentable appearance.

04

Students must wear the approved school uniform according to this schedule: Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday: regular school uniform; Wednesday and Friday: approved sportswear or games uniform.

05

Every student must be present at the assembly ground during morning assembly. No student is permitted to remain outside the assembly ground once assembly has begun without permission.

06

All students are expected to arrive at school by 7:15 a.m. Morning assembly must begin promptly at 7:30 a.m.

07

Any student who arrives at school at 8:00 a.m. or later shall be considered late and may face appropriate disciplinary action. The school gate shall be closed at 9:00 a.m., subject to the school's approved arrangements for late arrivals and emergencies.

08

Every student must proceed to the assembly ground promptly and be in the appropriate position before assembly begins. Students must remain orderly and attentive throughout the assembly.

09

Wearing any clothing or accessories that do not comply with the school's approved uniform policy is prohibited, except where permission has been granted by the school authorities.

10

All students must wear black shoes and black socks as part of the approved school uniform, unless otherwise authorized by the school.

🤝

2. Discipline and Good Conduct

Good behaviour, honesty, and accountability.

11

Every student must obey the school rules and regulations.

12

Students must respect the principal, teachers, non-teaching staff, prefects, and fellow students.

13

Fighting, bullying, intimidation, and threatening other students are prohibited.

14

Insulting, abusing, or using vulgar language against anyone in the school is prohibited.

15

Students must not engage in stealing, cheating, vandalism, or any form of dishonesty.

16

No student should take the law into their own hands when another student offends them.

17

Students must report serious misconduct to the appropriate teacher or school authority.

18

Students must not discriminate against others because of their background, disability, appearance, or financial circumstances.

19

Students must accept correction from teachers and other authorised school officials.

20

No student should deliberately disrupt lessons, assemblies, examinations, or other school activities.

3. Punctuality and Attendance

Being present, prepared, and on time.

21

Every student must arrive at school before the official starting time for morning assembly.

22

Students must attend morning assembly and other compulsory school activities.

23

Students must not leave the school premises during school hours without permission.

24

A student who is absent from school should provide an appropriate explanation through the school's accepted procedure.

25

Students must not skip lessons, examinations, or practical sessions without permission.

26

Students must return to their classrooms promptly after breaks.

27

Latecomers should follow the school's established procedure for reporting and addressing lateness.

Supporting learning, honesty, and academic progress.

28

Every student must come to school with the required books and learning materials.

29

Students must complete and submit assignments within the stipulated time.

30

Examination malpractice, including copying and exchanging answers, is prohibited.

31

32

36

Students must wear the approved school uniform on designated school days.

37

38

Keeping the school clean, safe, and welcoming.

Every student must help keep the classrooms, toilets, corridors, and school compound clean.

Littering the school premises is prohibited.

44

7. Prefects and Student Leadership

Leadership through service, fairness, and good example.

51

Every prefect must lead by example through good behaviour, punctuality, and respect.

52

Prefects must treat students fairly, regardless of friendship, class, popularity, or personal differences.

53

No prefect should use their position to intimidate, bully, insult, or exploit another student.

54

Prefects must not impose punishments that have not been authorised by the school.

55

Prefects must report serious offences to the appropriate teacher instead of taking matters into their own hands.

56

Prefects must not collect money, impose fines, or demand personal favours from students without proper school authorisation.

57

Students must cooperate with prefects when they are carrying out legitimate, authorised duties.

58

Prefects must listen to students' complaints respectfully and refer unresolved matters to the appropriate school authority.

59

No prefect should use their position to protect friends who break school rules.

60

The school should provide a fair procedure for students to report misconduct by prefects.

13. Fairness, Complaints, and Discipline

Consistent rules, fair hearings, and proportionate consequences.

93

School rules must be communicated clearly to students.

94

Disciplinary action should be proportionate to the seriousness of the offence.

95

Students should have an opportunity to explain their side of a disciplinary matter before a decision is made.

96

Teachers, prefects, and students should all be accountable for following the rules that apply to them.
